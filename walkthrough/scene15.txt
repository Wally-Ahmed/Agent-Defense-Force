Hermes publishes the incident, and that is the only thing Hermes is allowed to write. Five responders pick it up at once, each loading the security skills it thinks the case needs. Watch them come back — separately, by direct message, with no path between them. They will not agree perfectly. On this run four call it malicious and one will not go past suspicious, and that disagreement is not a failure of the design, it is the design working. What we refuse to do is let the first confident answer become the decision. Five assessments in. One deterministic decision out.
