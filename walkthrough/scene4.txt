Five responders, five harnesses, five processes. Something has to hand all of them the same case and collect five answers back. That is Cotal — an open-source agent mesh built on NATS. It is third-party. We did not author it, we use it as a dependency, and we credit the project. It gives us three delivery shapes: multicast to a channel, anycast to a service, and unicast direct message. The design turns on that last one. The incident goes out on a channel; every verdict comes back by direct message. So a responder is not merely asked to ignore its peers — it mechanically cannot see them. Independence is enforced by the transport, not by good manners.
