The target is called Northwind Projects: an ordinary multi-tenant project-management application, written in Jac. Tenants hold projects, projects hold tasks, tasks hold comments, and every membership carries a role — owner, admin, member, or viewer. Say the important part plainly. It is not deliberately vulnerable. Host-prefixed session cookies, double-submit CSRF, bearer tokens hashed with argon two i d, a fifteen-step deny decision order, per-route rate limits, restrictive CORS, and the full security header set. Nothing was weakened to make the demo work. If we had weakened something, the demo would prove nothing. The whole point is that a correctly secured application, behaving exactly as designed, still answers an agent holding a valid credential.
