Then we stop using models. The coordinator is not a sixth opinion and it is not an LLM judge — it is plain Jac with a fixed rule. Contain only if at least three of the five say malicious, and the weighted score clears one point five. Same inputs, same output, forever. It records how many voted each way, whether quorum was actually met, and whether the decision ran degraded because a responder timed out. A split does not get resolved by guessing: it triggers one bounded round of deliberation, and if it still will not settle, a human gets it. The exact weights are the one number in this design we have not fixed yet, and we would rather say so than invent one.
