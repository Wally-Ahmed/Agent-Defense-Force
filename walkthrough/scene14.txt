This is where correlation happens. The requests stop being log rows and become one incident: joined on the principal, the tenant, the session identifiers, the source addresses and the user agents, carrying the list of events that make up the evidence. On top of that join, the incident names what it is seeing — breadth, novelty, cadence, pivot after denial, escalation, canary contact — and the stages of a campaign those signals imply. That is the artifact the responders receive. Not a log excerpt, and not a paragraph we wrote for them. A structured incident record, with its evidence attached, that any of the five can walk for themselves.
